Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Family Assistant is a private app used by a single household. It is not offered to the public.

Google data

  • The app reads the household's own Google Calendar and adds or updates events on it when a family member asks.
  • Calendar data is used only to show the family's schedule and send them reminders.
  • Google user data is stored only on the household's own computers. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data

The AI model runs on the household's own machines. Optional backups are encrypted before they are stored off-site.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
